# Product Vision
## Community Campaign Platform
Positioning: **Create campaigns. Mobilize communities. Measure outcomes.**
Core principle: **Build for Tzu Chi. Architect for Expansion.**

First customer/founding partner: Tzu Chi Malaysia.
First campaign: 速素数数.

The MVP must feel Tzu Chi-specific in UX while the platform core remains generic for future environmental action, volunteering, fundraising, education and other campaigns.

Principles:
- Configuration-driven campaign behavior; do not hardcode vegetarianism into platform infrastructure.
- No pressure, comparison, grading, or historical reset: 不比较、不评判、不归零.
- Historical actions/achievements remain permanent; period progress may reset.
- Simple deployment does not mean simple design.
- Start simple. Design deeply. Scale deliberately.

Seven initial choices:
1. 随缘素
2. 欢喜素
3. 定日素
4. 一周素
5. 全月素
6. 发愿素
7. 已全素

Completion: `achieved / target * 100`; do not cap above 100%. 100%=Achieved, >100%=Exceeded.

Initial visualization may use generic `TREE`, displayed as 素心树. Do not create a hardcoded `SuXinTree` domain class.

MVP excludes: template builder, billing/payment, runtime AI, marketplace, advanced social/gamification, advanced verification, enterprise SSO, advanced BI, third-party integrations, public API marketplace.
